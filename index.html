Workflow strategy

Useful automations we could build around how joinery work actually comes in.

The monthly package includes up to four starter workflows. We would choose the most useful ones first, then add more in stages if you want to build the system out further.

New enquiry capture

When someone enquires through a form, message or call, create a contact, tag the job type and send an instant acknowledgement.

Quote follow-up sequence

If a quote has been sent but not accepted, send polite reminders after a few days so warm leads are not forgotten.

Review request workflow

Link to Google and ask happy customers for reviews after completed work, with a gentle follow-up if they do not reply.

Deposit and invoice prompts

Use simple reminders around deposit due, balance due and invoice status, with Xero kept in mind as the accountancy source.

Job stage updates

Keep customers reassured with short updates such as quote booked, materials ordered, start date confirmed and job completed.

Lost quote reactivation

Every month, check older unsold quotes and send a useful nudge to see whether they still want the work carried out.

Maintenance and repeat work

For past customers, send occasional reminders for repairs, upgrades, fitted storage, doors, flooring or seasonal work.

Referral prompt

After a happy customer leaves a review, ask whether they know anyone else looking for reliable joinery work.
